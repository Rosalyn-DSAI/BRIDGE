# A four-minute BRIDGE presentation

## Opening

“Important information is not always written in a way its reader can understand. Even after understanding it, we can forget to act. BRIDGE connects the message, the person, and the next step.”

## Show understanding

Open the Chinese onboarding notice and request simple English. Show the summary and the exception: staff who already completed training should provide proof instead of repeating it. Click **Show source**.

Say: “Each extracted instruction points back to the original message. We still ask the reader to review it.”

Switch the output to Spanish or Japanese and explain again. State whether this is a prepared sample or a live model result. Do not present fixtures as live AI.

## Show follow-through

Choose **Track this task**. Confirm an appropriate task title, deadline, time zone, and reminder time. In local mode, use quick-test times and explicitly say they are test dates.

Sign in through the email link and save. With live email configured, schedule the reminder near the beginning of the presentation so it arrives during the final minute. The worker must run continuously.

## Show responsible uncertainty

Open the Japanese AI report. Explain that the report contains an investigation instruction but no deadline. BRIDGE does not invent one. The user sets a follow-up date.

## Show the reminder

Open the actual inbox, or the clearly labeled local review inbox. Show the language, deadline, and task link. Complete the real-world demo action, then mark it done. If demonstrating cancellation, complete a second task before its reminder time and show the canceled status.

## Close

“BRIDGE helps people understand important messages across languages and turn the instructions they confirm into trackable actions.”

## Answers to likely questions

- **Why not just a chatbot?** “The project adds source-linked instructions, confirmed deadlines, saved task ownership, and scheduled follow-through.”
- **Is it always right?** “No. We check output structure and exact quote presence, preserve the source, and require review. We do not claim factual verification.”
- **Can it work in every sector?** “The communication workflow is reusable. Domain-specific reliability and integration need separate evaluation.”
- **Does it know I completed the external task?** “No. Completion is user-confirmed in this version.”
- **Does it send real emails?** “Yes when configured with our email provider and running worker. Local mode deliberately keeps messages inside the app.”
